## Platform

## Users
- Primary: Business owners, marketing directors, and general managers of mid-market companies and established regional brands in Da Nang and Central Vietnam (Hospitality, F&B, Retail, Automotive, EdTech).
- Situation: They have established business operations and product-market fit, but their digital marketing is fragmented, plateauing, or overly reliant on vanity metrics without clear financial attribution.
- Job to be done: Partner with a trusted, high-accountability growth studio that grounds brand positioning in measurable business outcomes (direct bookings, qualified leads, retail footfall, revenue).

## Product Purpose
Green Tree Agency is a Da Nang-based digital growth studio that builds and scales brands patiently from the roots up. Success means establishing sustainable, margin-focused growth for client partners through disciplined performance advertising, cohesive brand strategy, high-converting creative, and honest data attribution—moving past superficial clicks and vanity metrics.

## Positioning
"Brands aren't built overnight. They're grown."
Unlike transactional agencies that churn ad spend or pure creative studios detached from conversion metrics, Green Tree Agency operates as an integrated growth studio guided by the proprietary G.R.E.E.N. methodology (Ground, Root, Engage, Elevate, Nurture). Every marketing investment connects back to business margins, audience understanding, and compounding brand equity.

## Operating Context
- Client engagement cycle: Initial discovery call and marketing audit -> strategic roadmap & KPI alignment -> multi-channel campaign launch -> weekly iterative testing & margin optimization -> transparent monthly attribution reporting.
- Primary conversion workflow: Low-friction direct consultation booking via Zalo (primary business messaging channel in Vietnam), direct phone contact, or consultation inquiry form.
- Geographic & industry ecosystem: Central Vietnam economic hub (Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue) with strong concentration in premium hospitality, lifestyle F&B, authorized automotive dealerships, and educational institutions.

  1. Performance Ads (Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads)

## Product Principles
1. **Roots Before Leaves:** Strategy, positioning, and data infrastructure precede ad spend and campaign rollout.
2. **Margin Over Vanity:** Measure success in revenue, ROAS, CPA, and qualified pipeline—not impressions or vanity metrics.
3. **Compound Growth:** Cultivate sustainable brand momentum and customer retention rather than volatile short-term spikes.
4. **Radical Transparency:** Clear processes, weekly optimization rhythms, and honest attribution reporting without hidden markups.
5. **Craft in Every Touchpoint:** From micro-interactions to macro strategy, every touchpoint reflects deliberate, premium craftsmanship.
