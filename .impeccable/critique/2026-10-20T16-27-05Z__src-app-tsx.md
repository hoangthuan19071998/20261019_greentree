## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored for Green Tree, not category-interchangeable. The G.R.E.E.N. acrostic, growth-ring field, botanical language, heartwood/linen cadence, and slow motion form a coherent visual thesis. The main break occurs in Selected Work, which reads like a denser SaaS carousel placed inside a calm editorial agency site.

**Deterministic scan:** 32 advisory findings: 28 type-ramp deviations, 2 color deviations, 1 radius deviation, and 1 bounce-easing issue. The strongest concentration is `SelectedWorkSection.tsx`; additional findings appear in `Navbar.tsx`, `ServicesSection.tsx`, `ContactModal.tsx`, and `src/index.css`. Responsive alternatives inflate the raw type count, while the cream-palette signal is a brand false positive. The 12px radius, off-palette values, and `animate-bounce` align with real system drift.

**Visual overlays:** Injection succeeded in a fresh browser tab. The overlay reported two anti-patterns and additional signals for a hidden raster, numbered section labels, em-dash frequency, cream palette, and an infinite cue-dot animation. Cream and numbered labels are intentional brand choices; the useful follow-up is reduced-motion behavior and whether hidden imagery remains hidden.

## Overall Impression

The page earns attention through quiet authority and a specific visual metaphor. Its single largest opportunity is to make proof feel as credible and cultivated as the hero: repair the Vietnamese text, simplify interaction, and show enough context for the headline metrics to withstand scrutiny.

## What’s Working

1. **Specific hero:** “Ground / Root / Engage / Elevate / Nurture,” the ring field, patient-growth line, and 5.8× proof combine proposition and brand memory without generic agency hype.
2. **Strong long-page rhythm:** Heartwood hero, linen service journey, canopy process, linen proof, and heartwood contact create clear chapters and an emotionally coherent pace.
3. **Predictable conversion path:** “Book a discovery call” repeats consistently, and the close offers Zalo, email, and phone with explicit new-tab guidance.

## Priority Issues

### P0 — Repair corrupted Vietnamese text

**Why it matters:** Mojibake in case-study names, descriptions, metric symbols, and pagination labels is an immediate credibility and accessibility failure for a Vietnamese agency.

**Fix:** Re-save affected source as UTF-8, replace damaged strings with verified Vietnamese copy, and verify visible text plus accessible names in desktop and mobile browsers.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### P1 — Rebuild Selected Work inside the design system

**Why it matters:** Bold serif names, 12–16px radii, ad hoc greens, several shadows, monospaced URLs, badges, and dense metrics make the most important proof section feel like a separate product.

**Fix:** Return containers to 6px, use light serif typography, map colors to tokens, remove resting shadows, reduce badges, and make one outcome the hierarchy anchor.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`

### P1 — Simplify and stabilize case-study interaction

**Why it matters:** Header arrows, side arrows, dots, swipe, autoplay, full-card links, hover labels, and URLs create more than four simultaneous cues. Mobile also shows a short blank-value interval while entrance animation resolves.

**Fix:** Keep one control set, pause on focus and reduced motion, give the external destination a persistent label, and consider a static proof grid when three cards fit.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### P1 — Give visitors control over the horizontal service journey

**Why it matters:** About, an intro, and five services create a long, unfamiliar scroll contract before visitors reach methodology. Users cannot see its duration or jump directly to a relevant service.

**Fix:** Add a visible service index, current/total state, and a skip path on desktop; retain a simple vertical stack on touch and narrow screens.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### P2 — Reduce commitment anxiety at the final CTA

**Why it matters:** A cautious owner still does not know consultation length, response time, who they will meet, or whether the first audit is obligation-free.

**Fix:** Add one compact reassurance block beside the Zalo CTA covering what happens next and expected response time; keep factual claims verified.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

## Cognitive Load

Primary goal, value proposition, grouping, and macro hierarchy pass. The main failures are the >4 interaction cues in Selected Work, five peer service choices followed by five process stages, and the unconventional horizontal navigation contract. Progressive disclosure is mixed: the carousel hides proof while the service chapter forces sequential reveal.

## Emotional Journey

The opening creates quiet authority; methodology builds confidence; proof should be the peak but loses trust through encoding damage and visual drift. The warm contact ending recovers tone, yet it needs one low-risk next-step promise so visitors finish with confidence about commitment.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan, first-time owner:** The ROAS and CPA claims lack dates, baseline, spend band, or attribution window. Damaged Vietnamese text makes the claims feel unverified.

**Riley, deliberate evaluator:** The interface promises five disciplines and five stages but offers little evidence about deliverables, reporting, channel ownership, or how the numbers were attributed.

**Casey, distracted mobile visitor:** The horizontal journey and control-heavy one-card carousel slow rapid evaluation. The entrance animation briefly delays the mobile value proposition on load.

**Lan, GM of a premium Da Nang hotel:** Sel de Mer and direct-booking proof are highly relevant, but she needs seasonality, OTA-versus-direct attribution, the period behind 5.8×, and an unambiguously professional Vietnamese presentation.
